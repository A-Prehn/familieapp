Vores familie

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short: Vores familie stores your family's memories in your own iCloud account. We do not run servers that hold your data, we cannot read it, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

1. Who we are

Vores familie is developed and published by Andreas Prehn, Denmark ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the limited information described in this policy. Contact: andreas@prehn.net.

2. What the app is

Vores familie is a private family memory book. Parents can record milestones, photos, diary entries, highlights, events and holidays for their children, and share the family's book with the other parent.

3. What data is processed

The app processes only what you choose to enter:

  • Family name and the display name you choose for yourself.
  • Information about your children: name, date of birth or due date, gender (optional), milestones, free-text answers, diary entries, highlights and photos.
  • Family events, holidays and birthdays, including names and dates of other people you add.

This includes personal data about children. You, as parent or legal guardian, decide what to enter. We recommend only recording what you would be comfortable keeping in a physical memory book.

4. Where the data is stored

All content is stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit), in the private database belonging to your Apple ID. The data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. It is protected by your Apple ID credentials and two-factor authentication.

We do not operate our own servers and we have no technical access to your content. Apple acts as a data processor on your behalf under Apple's privacy terms.

If iCloud is unavailable, the app can run in a temporary local mode where data is kept in memory on the device only and is lost when the app is closed.

5. Sharing with the other parent

You may share your family's book with another person by creating an invite link. The link is generated by Apple's CloudKit sharing feature. Anyone who opens the link and accepts it with their Apple ID gains access to read and add content in that family. You can stop sharing at any time through iCloud sharing settings on your device.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or third-party trackers.
  • No account with us – you sign in with your Apple ID through iCloud.
  • No sale, rental or transfer of data to third parties.
  • No access to your photos beyond the ones you explicitly pick for the app.

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

Processing takes place on the basis of your consent and in order to provide the service you have requested (GDPR art. 6(1)(a) and (b)). Because the app is used for purely personal and family purposes, much of the content falls under the household exemption; this policy is nevertheless written to meet GDPR standards.

8. Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Access and export your data – it is in your own iCloud, and the app shows everything you have stored.
  • Correct entries directly in the app.
  • Delete content in the app, or delete all app data under iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Vores familie.
  • Withdraw consent by stopping use of the app and deleting its data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) or your local supervisory authority.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults (parents and guardians). It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data directly from children. Data about children is entered by their parents or guardians, who are responsible for it.

10. Retention

We retain nothing. Your content stays in your iCloud until you delete it or stop using iCloud.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. Material changes will be announced in the app.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: andreas@prehn.net.
